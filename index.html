What is the 2026 Round?

After more than a decade since the previous application round, ICANN opened a new opportunity to apply for and operate a top-level domain, based on community-developed policy recommendations and the 2026 Applicant Guidebook.

Brand TLDs

Organizations can seek a dedicated namespace for their brand and build controlled digital experiences around it.

Generic & community TLDs

Applications can address generic terms, industries, communities and other purposes, subject to ICANN rules.

Internationalized TLDs

The 2026 Round accepts applications in 27 scripts, supporting multilingual and non-Latin Internet naming.

Next milestone

Reveal Day

Following stages

Evaluation, contention & contracting

Applications may proceed through evaluations, objections, contention resolution, contracting and eventual delegation.

Registry Service Providers matter.
New gTLD applicants must use an evaluated Registry Service Provider (RSP) for technical registry operation, including DNS, DNSSEC, data escrow, abuse handling and operational resilience.
