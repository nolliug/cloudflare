ICANN · New Generic Top-Level Domains

The 2026 Round: applications are now being revealed.

The ICANN New gTLD Program: 2026 Round expands the Domain Name System with new top-level domains for brands, communities, geographic identities, generic terms and internationalized domain names.

Application window: 30 April — 12 August 2026

ICANN New gTLD ProgramSource: gTLD Club

What is the 2026 Round?

The second application round follows ICANN's 2012 round and gives eligible organizations an opportunity to apply to operate new top-level domains under the 2026 Applicant Guidebook.

Brand TLDs

Organizations can seek a dedicated namespace for their brand and build controlled digital experiences around it.

Generic & community TLDs

Applications can address generic terms, industries, communities and other purposes, subject to ICANN rules.

Internationalized TLDs

The round supports applications involving internationalized domain names and multiple scripts.

Applicant journey

The application window has closed. Applications now move through ICANN's processing and evaluation framework.

30 Apr — 12 Aug 2026

Application submission

Applicants submitted applications through ICANN's TLD Application Management System (TAMS).

Post-submission

Administrative processing

ICANN processes submitted applications and prepares the information required for subsequent program milestones.

Next milestones

Reveal, evaluation and objections

Application information will be published at the relevant milestone, followed by applicable evaluation, comment and objection processes.

Later stages

Contention, contracting & delegation

Where applicable, applications proceed through contention resolution, contracting, onboarding and eventual delegation.

Important distinction: An applicant announcement is not an ICANN determination that an application is complete, eligible, successful or approved. The definitive application record and evaluation status will come from ICANN's official 2026 Round processes.

Official resources

Use ICANN's official resources for authoritative requirements, application information and program updates.
