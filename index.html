Important: This is an independent test page. ICANN is the source of authority for the 2026 Round rules, application requirements, dates and program status. Information on this page should be checked against the current official ICANN documentation.

What is the second round?

The 2026 Round is the second application round of ICANN's New gTLD Program, following the 2012 round. It provides an opportunity for eligible organizations to apply for new top-level domains under the rules established for the 2026 Round.

Brand TLDs

Organizations can seek a dedicated namespace for their brand and build controlled digital experiences around it.

Generic & community TLDs

Applications can address generic terms, industries, communities and other purposes, subject to ICANN rules.

Internationalized TLDs

The 2026 Round supports applications involving internationalized domain names and multiple scripts.

Completed applications were submitted through ICANN's TLD Application Management System (TAMS).

Post-submission

Pre-Evaluation & Reveal Day

ICANN processes applications and publishes application information at the relevant program milestone.

Following stages

Evaluation, contention & contracting

Applications may proceed through community input, objections, string evaluation, contention resolution, applicant evaluation, contracting, onboarding and delegation.

Registry Service Providers matter.
New gTLD applicants must identify an evaluated Registry Service Provider (RSP) for technical registry operation. ICANN's RSP Evaluation Program separates technical evaluation of registry services from evaluation of the gTLD application itself.
