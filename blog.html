Vant Nexus announces applications for .PORTAL, .ISLAND and .TRUCK

Vant Nexus Technologies LLC has announced its participation in the 2026 ICANN New gTLD Round with applications for three new generic top-level domains: .PORTAL, .ISLAND and .TRUCK.

The announcement places Vant Nexus among the applicants publicly identifying their proposed strings following the close of the 2026 application window. According to Vant Nexus, the company is pursuing a registry strategy focused on practical domain-name usage, operational resilience and long-term commercial value.

Vant Nexus describes itself as a global digital infrastructure company headquartered in Dubai's Business Bay, with a footprint spanning the Middle East, Costa Rica, the United States and the Cayman Islands. Its stated approach is to connect each applied-for string to a broader project and business rationale rather than treating the TLD as an isolated namespace.

The company also identifies three replacement strings — .PROFILE, .PARK and .BEACH — in connection with its 2026-round portfolio.

Vant Nexus says its model is built around service-oriented registry operations, global registrar distribution and project-focused development. The company is led by senior executives including Chief Innovation Officer Jean Guillon, who brings experience from the domain industry and the 2012 ICANN new gTLD round.

As with other applicant announcements, the publication of these strings should not be interpreted as ICANN approval or confirmation that the applications have completed evaluation. The applications remain subject to the processes and requirements of the 2026 New gTLD Program.
